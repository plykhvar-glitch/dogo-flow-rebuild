What this is

We went through Dogo the way a new dog parent does, then rebuilt the few places we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion and revenue across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Dogo: walked your iOS onboarding from the first screen to sign-up, read your App Store listing and dogo.app, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people at the moment they decide.

Your flow is already strong. It asks good questions and it really uses them: the loader names the breed and the age, and the plan picks courses from the topics. So what follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each is built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the paw header and yellow progress line, the birthday wheel, the mint loader with its ring, the course cards, the yellow button, the plan cards with their save badges. The illustrations come from your own App Store screenshots. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but show too late.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
