# Growth Audit — Dogo

**Live:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/dogo-flow-rebuild/
**Prototype on a phone:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/dogo-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ranked ideas for Dogo's iOS onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype that rebuilds the screens they land on in Dogo's own design: the paw header, the birthday wheel, the mint loader, the course cards, the yellow button and the plan cards. Only the sequence changes.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Name the dog and the plan on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Offer one month, no trial, when they close | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 03 | Move your proof up to the plans | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications when the reminder needs them | Welcome, trial reminder, sign-up | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put the training time on the plan | Training time, plan | CR +5–10% |

The prototype has Light / Dark, EN / DE / PL, a 30-second walkthrough, and jump chips that land on any screen with answers filled in.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, quote, award and course line comes from Dogo's own App Store listing, dogo.app and in-app screens. Illustrations and the welcome photo are taken from Dogo's App Store screenshots. Store prices are shown as published in US dollars in every language.

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Dogo.*

python3 -m http.server 8000
